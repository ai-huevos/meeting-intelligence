# Sales Playbook: Seeds, Nets, Spears

---

## Seed Framework (Cold Outreach)

### 1. Target Identification
- **ICP:** Ideal Customer Profile
- **Signals:** LinkedIn activity, company news, funding rounds
- **Data:** Apollo, Crunchbase, personal network

### 2. The "Ask" (Not Pitch)
**Mal:** "We offer AI automation services for your operations..."
**Bien:** "I see you just raised Series A. Congrats. How are you thinking about ops scaling as you grow to 50 people?"

### 3. Value Prop (30-60s)
**Pattern:** [Observation] + [Question] + [Hook]

```
[Observación]: "You're building a remote-first team in LatAm"
[Pregunta]: "How are you managing cultural alignment across 3 countries?"
[Hook]: "We built an OS that automates your cadence so founders reclaim 20+ hours/week"
```

### 4. Next Step (Not Meeting Always)
**Opciones:**
- "Would you be open to a 10-min chat?" (Low friction)
- "Who owns ops decisions at [Company]?" (Gatekeeper ID)
- "Send me your calendar link" (If signal is strong)

## Net Framework (Warm Network)

### 1. Source Activation
- **LinkedIn:** Comment on posts, mutual connections
- **Events:** LatAm tech meetups, conferences
- **Referrals:** "Who else should I talk to in your network?"

### 2. Warm Intro
**Template para hacer intros:**
```
Hi [Founder A],

I'd love to connect you with [Founder B].

[Founder A]: Built [Company X], knows [Context Y]
[Founder B]: Building [Company Z], solving [Problem]

Context: [Why the intro makes sense]

Are you open to an intro? If yes, I'll forward both emails.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 3. Follow-up Cadence
- **Day 1:** Reply to intro
- **Day 3:** If no reply, bump gently
- **Day 7:** Final check, then drop

## Spear Framework (Target Account)

### 1. Account Selection
**Criteria:**
- Series A/B funded
- 25-100 employees
- LatAm focus
- Pain point: "grew faster than systems"

### 2. Multi-Threading
**No single point of failure:**
- [ ] CEO (via LinkedIn)
- [ ] COO/Ops (via LinkedIn)
- [ ] VP Engineering (via warm intro)
- [ ] HR (via events)

### 3. Value Ladder
**Sequence:**
1. **Discovery:** "What's your #1 ops challenge?"
2. **Insight:** "I see you're scaling fast. Most founders hit SPOF at 30 people..."
3. **Proof:** "Here's a case study: [Link]"
4. **Call to Action:** "Can we schedule a 20-min demo?"

### 4. Account Metrics (Weekly)
- [ ] New contacts added
- [ ] Reply rate %
- [ ] Calls scheduled
- [ ] Pipeline stage movement

## Closing Framework

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | "Cost = 1% of payroll. Time saved = 20 hrs/founder/week. ROI < 2 weeks" |
| "We have systems" | "Great. What % of time is spent updating them vs shipping product?" |
| "Not a priority" | "Fair. What's your timeline for ops scaling to 100 people?" |
| "Need to ask CTO" | "Understood. Who should I copy on the email?" |

### RAPID Decision
- **R**ecommender: [Founder name proposing deal]
- **A**gree: [Founder signing]
- **P**erform: [Founder deploying contract]
- **I**nput: [Founder who consulted]
- **D**ecide: [Founder who signs]

---

## Pipeline Tracking

**Stages:**
1. Target → 2. Contacted → 3. Discovery → 4. Proposal → 5. Negotiation → 6. Contract → 7. Closed

**Required fields por stage:**
- **Target:** Company name, size, signals
- **Contacted:** Date, channel, message sent
- **Discovery:** Pain points identified, budget, timeline
- **Proposal:** Value prop, pricing, terms
- **Negotiation:** Objections, next steps
- **Contract:** Draft version, RAPID owners
- **Closed:** ARR amount, close date, onboarding start

**Métrica objetivo:** 45-90 días desde Target → Close
