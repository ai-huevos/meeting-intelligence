# Recruiting "Who" Method

Basado en Geoff Smart *Who*

---

## Scorecard (Role Definition)

```yaml
role: "VP Engineering"
mission: "Escalar el producto a 10K usuarios MRR"
outcomes:
  - "Líder técnico reconocido por el mercado"
  - "Team de 10 ingenieros de alto rendimiento"
  - "Productos con >99.9% uptime"
what_you_must_do:
  - "Hire y desarrollar equipo"
  - "Definir arquitectura técnica"
  - "Aprobar technical decisions"
success_criteria:
  - "Product uptime >99.9%"
  - "Team velocity: 2 story points/engineer/week"
  - "Retention <10% anual"
```

## 90-Day Roadmap

### Day 1-30: Onboarding & Assessment
- [ ] Objetivos alineados con CEO
- [ ] Scorecard definida y aprobada
- [ ] Stakeholders interviews completadas
- [ ] Technical assessment completado

### Day 31-60: Execution & Quick Wins
- [ ] Primer deliverable shipping
- [ ] Team structure definida
- [ ] Processes documentados
- [ ] Primer checkpoint con CEO (Day 45)

### Day 61-90: Scale & Evaluation
- [ ] Segundo deliverable shipping
- [ ] Team hiring completado
- [ ] Performance review
- [ ] Final checkpoint con CEO (Day 90)
- [ ] Decisión: extend, pivot, o terminate

## Reference Call Template

**Opening:** "Hi [Name], [Candidate] is considering joining [Company] as [Role]. You've been listed as a reference. Can we speak for 10-15 minutes?"

**Questions:**
1. **Strengths:** "What are [Candidate]'s top 3 strengths?"
2. **Weaknesses:** "What areas did [Candidate] struggle with?"
3. **Management:** "How did they manage [specific situation]?"
4. **Rehire:** "Would you rehire? Why/why not?"
5. **Context:** "What context should I know about their role/company?"

**Closing:** "Any questions for me? Thanks for your time."
